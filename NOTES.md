## Features

- **Onboarding** — first run: welcome → bank SMS → notification access → alerts → optional monthly income. Each permission is explained before Android asks; every step has "Not now". Adds 10 starter categories. "Reset everything" shows it again. The welcome step also offers "Moving from another phone? Restore a backup" (file picker; Drive restore needs the OAuth client): the last step lists what came back and skips the income question if the backup had one; starter categories are added only if the backup had none. The restore runs in `OnboardingViewModel` (survives rotation) and its result is kept in prefs until setup finishes (`RestoredInfo`). Whether setup shows is decided by the "finished" flag alone; the "has data, so it's an old install" check runs only on the first launch (`SetupGate`), so data added mid-setup (a restore, an SMS) doesn't end setup early.
- **Auto-tracking** — parses bank SMS (`SmsReceiver`, `SmsParser`) and GPay/PhonePe/Paytm notifications (`TransactionNotificationListener`, `NotificationParser`). Credits, promos, OTPs, bill/statement reminders, "will be debited" notices, mandate registrations and failed payments are ignored; WhatsApp is not read. SMS + notification for the same transaction are deduped on amount within 3 minutes, (`CaptureDuplicates`, shared by both paths). An expense added by hand or an auto-logged bill counts only if it names the same payee, whole words (`samePayee`: "Crunchyroll" absorbs a CRUNCHYROLL SMS; "YouTube" or "Lunch" doesn't). With no categories a payment is saved as pending, never dropped. Settings → Auto-tracking card shows whether SMS and notification access are on and fixes each in one tap.
- **Capture notification** — three category buttons: the auto-detected category, then the most used in the last 60 days, then alphabetical (`QuickCategories`). Tapping the notification opens the full picker.
- **Overview (Dashboard)** — month navigation, budget warning (pending expenses count toward total), upcoming bills, EMI commitments, savings goals, Cash Flow Forecast (income − spent − bills still due − EMI still due = free to spend; current month only, when income is set). The income is the budget month's forecast income (`IncomeForecast`, see Income). The 6-month trend chart was removed on purpose. Months follow the **month start day** (below).
- **Expenses** — search, category filter chips, multi-tagging, edit expense, date picker. Manual entry (F1): labelled "Add expense" button; the amount field takes sums like `120+45` or `3×40` (`AmountCalculator`, with `+ − × ÷` chips that insert at the cursor and an "= ₹165" preview); category is optional ("Choose later (pending)") and starts on the last one used; Edit → **Duplicate** copies an expense dated now (no SMS text copied). Filters dialog has a **Dates** section (`DatePresetOptions`, shared with CSV export); the filter is a `DateFilter` (a preset, or chosen dates), so "This month" moves on with the month; an active date filter shows as a chip that clears it. Filters are kept in `SavedStateHandle`, so they survive Android stopping the app in the background. Statistics' "View all" hands its dates over as a one-time request in `AppNavGraph` (`expensesRangeRequest`), not route arguments, since returning to the saved Expenses tab restores its old arguments.
- **Income (F4)** — income lives in its own tables (`incomes`, `recurring_incomes`, `recurring_income_months`, DB v14), so every existing sum, budget and alert still means spending. Expenses has a **Spending | Income** switch (kept in `SavedStateHandle`): the Income list is grouped by day with green "+₹", Edit / Duplicate / Save & duplicate / Delete; search, the date filter and amount limits apply to it, category and tag chips don't (they're hidden). The add dialog has an Expense/Income switch; income has no category or payment method. **Recurring income** (Settings → Bills & Reminders, e.g. Salary on the 1st) is logged by `RecurringManager.applyRecurringIncome` on its calendar day like auto-log bills at launch, right after it's added, edited or switched on, and after a restore. Each month it handles is recorded in `recurring_income_months` in the same transaction as the entry, so deleting or moving the entry doesn't make the month log again (`byHand` marks a month it skipped because the income was already entered by hand: same name and amount, like bills; alike recurring incomes share hand entries one each, earliest due day first; see `RecurringIncomeRule`). Deleting a recurring income keeps what it logged, detached, so adding it back doesn't log the month twice. Income tags are searchable; the tag chips stay spending-only. **Forecast income** (`IncomeForecast`): for a budget month, income logged in it plus enabled recurring income due from today to the month's end that `RecurringManager` will still log: due from the start of this calendar month and not done by the same `RecurringIncomeRule` and month records (so a salary whose day passed while the app was closed counts in the widget, and a hand-entered or deleted one doesn't); if both are 0, the old setting, now called **"Expected monthly income"**, so nothing changes until income is logged. The Overview, its Cash Flow card and both widgets (`BudgetPulseRepository`) use it. Statistics (Monthly and Range) shows Income · Spent · Saved (coral when negative) when the period has logged income. Onboarding's income question still sets the expected figure. The Overview and Settings watch the expected figure (`PreferencesManager.monthlyIncomeFlow`, a SharedPreferences listener), so a restore, a reset or setup changing it shows at once; each used to keep a copy read when it opened, so the Overview showed the old income until the app restarted.
- **Subscriptions** (#8) — repeating payments detected from history (`SubscriptionDetector`): Overview card (top 3 + yearly total) and a Subscriptions page with "Remind me" (adds a bill) and "Not a subscription" (with Undo). A missed or extra payment ends a run of payments instead of hiding the subscription for good; the payments after it start a new run (a monthly one is listed again after 3). The payment that ends a run can start one at another cadence (monthly, then quarterly), but that run needs one more payment than usual, so a pause (5 Mar, then 5 Jun) isn't read as quarterly.
- **Subscription price changes (F5)** — the detector follows a subscription's payments into another price at its cadence (a rise over 10% used to split it in two and show the old price), and reports the latest change (`previousAmount`, `priceChangedOn`, `priceChange`) for 2 billing cycles. Rules: amounts within ₹1 or 2% of the payment before are the same price, 5% for monthly and longer charges with paise (converted from dollars, they move with the exchange rate; a slow drift never adds up to a change; weekly ones with paise are shopping). Finding a quarterly/yearly subscription (2 payments) still needs amounts within 2%, paise or not; the old price must have been paid as often as a new subscription needs (3 monthly, 2 yearly), so a one-month blip isn't a change and going back isn't alerted; a jump over 2× or under ½ is another purchase or plan; a new price charged more than 2 days before the renewal is an extra purchase, not the renewal (the renewal is a calendar cycle after the last payment, or sooner if the payments came that much sooner: 28-day prepaid recharges); these checks apply to small (same amount group) changes too; a change that fails them isn't reported, and the subscription stays listed (a single doubtful payment is left out while the subscription is current without it); a payee with other purchases in the 2 cycles before (Amazon orders, or a second subscription billed a day apart, like iCloud and Apple Music on one "APPLE.COM/BILL") doesn't get changes; a payment within 2 days counts as the same charge only at the same price. Subscriptions cards show "↑ ₹50 since 5 Oct" (coral) or "↓ …" (jade), from the prices as shown in whole rupees, like the alert (`formatRupees` is in `ui/util/Rupees.kt`); the Overview card marks rises ("↑ price up"). **One notification** when the new price is first captured ("Netflix price went up / ₹499 → ₹549 a month · ₹600 more a year"), channel `subscription_alerts`, tag `price_change`, id = masked key hash; tapping it opens Subscriptions through `LaunchAction.OpenSubscriptions` (own PendingIntent action, so it never collides). All alerting goes through `PriceChangeAlerts.checkAfterCapture` (Pro gating goes there later), called after the insert in `SmsReceiver` (after the whole SMS batch is saved and the widget updated), `TransactionNotificationListener` and `ExpensesViewModel` add/duplicate (also the widget "+") and edits that change the payee or amount (a capture saved as "UPI" renamed to "Netflix"; checked in `ExpenseRepository.update`, so the Expenses edit, the Categorize amount fix and a message-rule correction all are), each in `runCatching`; never on other edits, restores, mock data or "Recurring" entries. It runs on IO, under `NonCancellable`, skips payees detection leaves out ("UPI", "HDFC debit", short names, bills) before loading anything, loads this payee's history only (a `LIKE` on the key's longest run of a–z/0–9, since SQLite's `LIKE` ignores case for ASCII only, then an exact key match), and alerts only if the expense is dated within 2 days of now and of the change (a back-dated entry doesn't). The change is claimed under a lock and `commit()`ed before posting (`price_alert_prefs`: one entry per payee, `price_alert_<key>` = when its last alerted change was first charged), so SMS + UPI for one charge alert once, and the same charge saved again a little earlier (by hand, a late SMS) doesn't alert again (within 2 days is the same change); it's stored even without notification permission. Not in backups (a restore isn't a capture). Settings → Preferences → "Subscription price alerts" (`priceAlertsEnabled`, default on), in backups as an optional setting (older files leave it alone). The Overview's state is now computed off the main thread (it runs detection over all history).
- **Message rules** (#9) — teach a bank message the parser reads wrong ("It's a payment" / "Not a payment"), from an expense or by pasting text; rules are checked before the built-in parser and included in backups. Long multi-part SMS are joined before parsing.
- **Statistics** — Monthly (budget month), Annual (calendar year) and **Range** (last 3 / 6 budget months, all time, or chosen dates; compared with the same number of days before) views. A period that's still running ("this month", "last 3 months") is compared at today's pace (`BudgetPeriod.comparableTotal`), on the card and in the insight alike, and says so ("vs this point last month"); "last N months" is compared with the N budget months before (`monthsBefore`). The Range view has its own flow (`rangeState`), only collected while it's shown, and both flows run off the main thread. Tapping a category shows its last 6 budget months and its subcategory split for the period on screen. Smart insights (weekend vs weekday, no-spend days), auto-tracking stats. Monthly and Range share `periodStats` / `PeriodView`.
- **Month start day (F3)** — Settings → Preferences → "Month starts on" (1–28; 1 = calendar month), stored in `PreferencesManager.monthStartDay`, read live through `MonthStartDay`, included in backups (`BackupSettings.monthStartDay`, optional so the format stays v2), reset to the 1st by Reset everything. `BudgetPeriod` is the one place that turns it into date ranges; the Overview, the monthly Statistics view, the widget, budget alerts, CSV and Expenses "This/Last month" use it. The widget's "vs" line compares at today's pace too ("vs SEP PACE"). Bills, EMIs, bill reminders, the annual view and the 12-month chart stay calendar-based; `CashFlow` counts calendar due dates that fall inside the budget month (a 25th–24th month includes a bill due on the 5th). Budget alerts keep the old key for start day 1, so updating doesn't repeat this month's alerts.
- **Budgets** — per-category limits (Settings → Category Budgets, or the Categories tab), alerts at a customizable threshold (slider) and at 100%.
- **Settings** — Auto-tracking, Bills & Reminders (bills, recurring income, loans/EMIs), Category Budgets, Cloud Backup (Back up now, Restore from Drive), Preferences (budget alerts, week start, month start, privacy mode, App Lock, widget), Data & Backup (export, CSV export, restore from file, reset; "Load mock data" in debug builds only).
- **Backup** — format v3 includes categories, expenses, bills, loans, savings goals, learned merchants, income records, recurring income and its handled months, the expected income and alert settings. Restore checks the file first (NaN amounts, missing text, a recurring day outside 1–31 are refused), shows what it contains, and runs in one transaction. v1 and v2 files still restore and leave the sections they don't have (v2: income) alone; v4+ is refused. Setup's restore counts income records as having income. Captured SMS text is never included.
- **CSV export** — Settings → Data & Backup → Export CSV: this month, last month, this year, all time or a custom range (`ExportRange`). `CsvExporter` writes RFC 4180 with a UTF-8 BOM (Excel shows ₹ correctly), oldest first; text starting with = + - @ gets a leading apostrophe so spreadsheets don't run it as a formula; columns date, time, type, amount, description, category, subcategory, payment_method, tags, source. Income rows (`type = income`, no category or payment method) are merged in by date. Saved through the system file picker.
- **App Lock** — fingerprint, face or screen lock; re-locks after 30 s in the background (`AppLockState`); lock screen with Unlock button instead of closing the app. A dialog asked for while locked isn't created until unlock (it would be a newer window on top of the lock); one already open stays under the lock with its text. Screens open dialogs through `ui/components/AppDialogs.kt` (`AppAlertDialog`, `AppDatePickerDialog`, `AppModalBottomSheet`), never the Material/Compose ones directly: `AppDialogsUsageTest` (in `testDebugUnitTest`, not the build) fails otherwise. Google's Drive consent screen also waits for unlock.
- **Privacy** — captured SMS/notification text is deleted after 7 days (`CapturedTextRetention`); Android's automatic Google backup excludes all app data; privacy mode hides the app in recents from startup.
- **Other** — recurring bills auto-logged on their due day, bill reminders (WorkManager), Budget Pulse home-screen widget (its "+" opens the add-expense dialog, F1; launch intents go through `LaunchAction`; `WidgetUpdateHelper.update` is a suspend function that asks the system for the widget ids on `Dispatchers.IO` (that call can stall, and froze the app when made on the main thread) and waits for it, so an SMS receiver's refresh is done before the receiver finishes; changing the expected income refreshes it too), savings goals with deposit dialog, merchant learning with whole-word matching, new app icon, dark splash background (`#171511`).

### Known trade-offs

- A bill due today counts as "still due" even if already paid.
- Two auto-log bills with the same name, amount and month log only once.
- Two different payments of the same amount within 3 minutes, one seen only by SMS and the other only by a payment app, are saved as one (the capture dedupe matches on amount, since the two sources name payees differently).
- Auto-logged bills are only added when the app is opened (dated correctly, even if opened late).
- Recurring income, like auto-log bills, is logged when the app opens (or when it's changed in Settings), dated its day. Its month records stop a deleted salary from coming back, including after a restore (they're in the backup). Like bills, a day missed in a month the app wasn't opened isn't logged later. Trade-off: any income logged makes the forecast ignore the expected figure, even a small one-off (as the plan specifies).
- Income dates are instants, like expenses: a salary logged at midnight on 1 Oct in India shows on 30 Sep in New York, and that month's forecast follows.
- Existing expenses keep the old, broken descriptions from before #2; only learned rules were cleaned.
- Price changes (F5): a change is reported only if the old price was paid at least as often as a new subscription needs (3 monthly, 2 quarterly/yearly). A change of 2% or less (or ₹1) isn't one. A change over 2× or under ½ (a plan upgrade 199 → 649) isn't followed: the subscription drops off until the new price has been paid 3 times, as before. At a payee you also shop from (another purchase in the 2 cycles before), no change is reported, however small. A big change that goes back the next month is reported going up, not coming back. The alert uses the card's name, title-cased when it's all capitals ("NETFLIX" → "Netflix", "YouTube Premium" kept), and whole rupees like the cards. A one-off order at a subscription's payee on or just before its renewal day, at a different price within 2×, still reads as a price change. If the bank SMS and the payment app report one charge with slightly different amounts and the off one arrives first, it can alert for that small difference (only if over 2%).
- A missed payment restarts a subscription's count, so a monthly one disappears until 3 payments in a row again (it used to disappear for good).
