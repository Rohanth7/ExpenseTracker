# Thriftpot privacy policy

*Effective: [DATE OF FIRST RELEASE]*

Thriftpot is an expense tracker for Android, made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what the app reads, what it keeps, and where your data goes.

The short version: Thriftpot has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Your data stays on your phone. It only leaves the phone when you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive or export it to a file.

## What Thriftpot reads, and why

**Bank SMS (if you allow it).** When a new SMS arrives, Thriftpot checks it on your phone. If the message is from a bank or payment service and says money was debited, the app logs the amount, the payee and the date as an expense. Thriftpot does not read your SMS history. It ignores one-time passwords (OTPs), offers, and personal messages. The text of a captured message is kept on your phone for 7 days, so you can check what was logged, and is then deleted. Message text is never included in backups.

**Payment app notifications (if you allow it).** Some UPI payments only show up as a notification from the payment app. If you turn on notification access, Thriftpot reads notifications from a fixed list of payment and banking apps, on your phone, to log the payment: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, CRED, MobiKwik, Freecharge, and the HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, RBL and Yes Bank apps. It ignores notifications from every other app, including messaging and chat apps.

**What you enter.** Expenses, income, categories, budgets, bills, loans, savings goals and settings that you add in the app.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Android keeps it away from other apps.

## What leaves your phone

**Nothing goes to us.** We have no servers and we don't receive your data.

**Google Drive backup (optional, off by default).** If you connect a Google account under Settings, then Cloud Backup, Thriftpot saves a backup file to a hidden app folder in *your own* Google Drive. Only Thriftpot can see that folder. The file contains your expenses, income, categories, bills, loans, savings goals, message rules and app settings. It does not contain the text of any SMS or notification. It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to Google. Google stores it under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Thriftpot asks Google only for permission to use its own app folder. It cannot see your other Drive files.

**Files you export.** Backups and CSV exports are saved where you choose with Android's file picker. What happens to them after that is up to you.

**Google Play.** Google Play may collect crash and performance reports about the app under Google's own policies. We don't add any analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code to the app.

We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone. We don't use it for advertising, credit scoring or lending.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Receive SMS | Log debits from bank SMS as they arrive. Optional. |
| Notification access | Log UPI payments from payment and banking app notifications (the list above). Optional. |
| Notifications | Let you pick a payment's category in one tap, and send budget alerts and bill reminders. Optional. |
| Biometric | App Lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock. Optional. |
| Internet | Google Drive backup only. |

The app works without any of the optional permissions. You can add expenses by hand.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.

- To delete everything on the phone, use Settings, then Data & Backup, then Reset everything, or uninstall the app.
- To delete the Google Drive backup, open Google Drive, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Thriftpot, and choose "Delete hidden app data".

Because we never receive your data, we can't see, change or delete it for you.

## Security

App data is stored in Thriftpot's private storage and protected by your phone's encryption. You can also turn on App Lock, which asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock to open the app. You can also turn on privacy mode, which hides the app's content in the recent apps screen. With App Lock or privacy mode on, Thriftpot's notifications are kept off your lock screen.

## Children

Thriftpot is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly handle children's data.

## Your rights

You have full control of your data on your phone: you can view, correct, export and delete all of it in the app. If you live somewhere with privacy laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the EU or UK GDPR, or California's CCPA, those rights apply. Because we don't collect or hold your data, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. We don't sell or share personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you before it takes effect.
